Warehouse rework and reserve storage | EUR
Per completed staged-box work order: 18.00 for retrieval, identity scan and putaway (12 staff minutes), plus 2.00 per reserved day, rounded up from cutoff to booked collection or saved expiry if earlier. The known two-day collection booking costs 22.00. Costs are charged to the centre, never the owner.
Booked warehouse cover completes all valid date entries saved by 13:30 at 13:45. Reserve capacity is available for the full batch; rework does not delay the carrier or other collections. A date edit commits the work order; completed work produces scans and an invoice entry. Cancelling a queued order before completion incurs no charge. Repeated edits do not duplicate a work order. Written managerial permission is required before commitment (OPS-14 section 4).
