Its job is to confirm the schedule as set out in the team procedure and keep records accurate.

Under the Income Team procedure only the Income Manager can authorise a hold, in writing. The scheduling tool lets the assistant put any case on hold with a free-text reason. A held case is taken off the schedule, the bailiff office is asked to postpone it, and the hold is listed with its reason in the weekly deviation report that the Income Manager and internal audit review. The tool works only until the schedule is submitted at 15:00; after that, only the Income Manager can postpone a booked eviction, by phoning the bailiff office.
